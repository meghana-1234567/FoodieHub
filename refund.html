Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand our proposed refund and cancellation guidelines.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy describes the proposed cancellation and refund guidelines for FoodieHub. The current website is a demonstration project and does not accept real orders or payments.

2. Order Cancellation

  • Customers should request cancellation as soon as possible.
  • For a future real ordering service, cancellation eligibility will depend on the order preparation and dispatch status.
  • Orders that have already been prepared or dispatched may not be eligible for cancellation, subject to applicable consumer rights.

3. Refund Eligibility

For a future real ordering service, refund requests may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • Payment deducted but the order was not confirmed.
  • Order cancellation accepted under the applicable cancellation rules.
  • Incorrect or missing items, subject to verification.
  • Food quality issues reported within a reasonable period.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of preference after an order has been prepared.
  • Incorrect address or contact details supplied by the customer, where this prevents delivery.
  • Requests that do not meet the applicable refund conditions.

These conditions are subject to applicable consumer protection laws and individual circumstances.

5. Refund Processing

If a real payment service is introduced, approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method where possible. Processing times will depend on the payment provider and bank.

6. How to Request a Refund

In a future live service, customers should provide their order reference, registered email address and a description of the issue through the customer support channel.

7. Contact Support

For questions about cancellations or refunds, contact hello@foodiehub.com.

8. Policy Changes

FoodieHub may update this policy when actual ordering, delivery or payment services are introduced. Updated terms will be published on this page.
