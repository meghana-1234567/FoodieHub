Privacy Policy

Learn how FoodieHub handles information.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the FoodieHub demonstration website handles information when visitors browse and interact with its features.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, information may include:

  • Name, email address and a password hash when you register. Passwords are not stored in plain text.
  • Account sign-in state in a session cookie and the time of a successful login.
  • Food selections saved in your browser's local storage.
  • Contact and review form entries are not sent or stored by this demo.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To create accounts, verify sign-ins and maintain login sessions.
  • To display and maintain shopping cart selections.

Contact and review form contents are not transmitted or stored because those demo forms do not submit data.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The site uses browser local storage to remember cart items and a session cookie for account sign-in. Cart data can be cleared through browser settings; signing out clears the login session.

5. Data Security

When the Flask backend is running, registration details are stored in the configured SQLite or MySQL database, with passwords protected using a one-way hash. Login sessions use a cookie. This project is a local demo, not a production service; do not use a password you rely on elsewhere.

6. Sharing of Information

Account details are stored by the configured backend. Contact and review forms only display a demo confirmation and do not send or save their contents. Cart data remains in your browser. The demo does not process orders or payments, and no third-party form or payment service is configured.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise manage your personal information. Requests can be directed to the website administrator.

8. Children's Privacy

The website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization and safeguards.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. Changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, email hello@foodiehub.com.
